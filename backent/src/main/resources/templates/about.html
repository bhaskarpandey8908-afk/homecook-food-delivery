About the HomeChef App

HomeChef is a community-based food delivery platform created to make homemade meals accessible, easy to order, and reliable for customers who want authentic taste and healthy food.

100% Homemade

Fresh food by local chefs for local families.

App Details

What this app does

This app allows customers to browse meals, create accounts, log in, and order homemade food from nearby chefs. The platform is designed for convenience, safety, and local community-based food discovery.

Platform Focus

Core features

  • User registration and login
  • Meal listing and browsing
  • Chef and customer roles
  • Cart and order workflow
  • Local homemade food discovery
Design Style

Visual identity

The app uses warm food-inspired colors such as orange, cream, and brown, with clean layouts and bold cards to create trust, appetite, and a friendly modern design.
