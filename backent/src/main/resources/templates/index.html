About HomeChef

HomeChef is a food delivery platform built for customers who want nutritious, homemade, and authentic meals prepared by skilled home chefs. The system helps users discover meals, register securely, log in, and order from trusted local cooks.

The project is designed around a simple user flow: customer browses meals, creates an account, logs in, and places orders. Chefs can prepare and manage their meals while administrators can monitor the product catalog and operations.

App Purpose

Connect home chefs with hungry customers in a community-driven food ecosystem.

Design Concept

Warm colors, fresh food visuals, trust-focused layout, and simple user navigation.
